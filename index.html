MAP THE SCRAP

Hello, we are 'Map the Scrap', an initiative to curb the affects of direct extraction mining that goes into general process of making the mobile phones.


The materials discussed in the first page, their extraction and processing takes upto 20% of global Green House Gases Emissions. Which is a huge impact on the environment inducing climate change. A solution we found to reduce this impact is choosing the path of circular economy.


What Is Circular Economy?

  • It uses secondary grade or EoL-Mps (End-of-Life Mobile Phones).
  • Redcuing almost more than 95% of these emissions impact on first-hand extraction of metals used in making mobile phones.

Now, this impact is an everyday process which you & we shall contribute to.


Wondering how you can help us? Simple! Follow these steps:

  • Click here 'Solutions', and find the form in the solutions page to help us locate these scrap materials in your region.

But, how does this help you?

You will get:

  1. A certificate of recognition from the company.
  2. A certification of recognition from our affiliated network who also contribute to this change.

Let's help the world become a better place. For you, us & for the generations to come.
